# Exporo

> Exporo is a Hamburg, Germany-based digital investment platform for real estate and renewable-energy infrastructure. It operates as a regulated European Crowdfunding Service Provider (ECSP), letting private investors participate in property development and clean-energy projects. Exporo has mediated over €1.17 billion of capital across more than 600 financed projects. No public developer API or developer portal is published at this time.
